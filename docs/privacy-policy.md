# Mathly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-14_

Mathly ("we", "our") is an AI math tutor app. This policy explains what data
the app handles and why. The short version: your problem photos go to our AI
service to be solved, nothing is sold, and you can delete everything at any
time from the app's Settings.

## What the app stores on your device

Stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage), never uploaded as-is:

- Your onboarding answers (subjects, level, goal, explanation style)
- Solved problems, step-by-step solutions, and follow-up chats
- Subscription status

Choosing **Settings → Delete all my data** removes all of it immediately.

## What is sent to our servers

When you scan or type a problem:

- The photo (or typed text) of the problem, so our AI can read and solve it
- Your level and explanation-style preferences, so the answer is pitched right
- Follow-up questions and the problem context needed to answer them

Photos are transmitted over HTTPS, used only to produce your solution, and are
not retained after the request completes. Chat history is stored on your
device only — we do not keep a server-side copy.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple App Store / Google Play. We
receive anonymized purchase status (via RevenueCat) to unlock Pro features.
We never see or store your payment details.

## What we do NOT do

- No ads
- No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing
- No tracking across other apps or websites
- No account required — no name, email, or phone number needed to use Mathly

## Children's privacy

Mathly is rated 3+ / Everyone and asks for no personal information from any
user, including children.

## Your rights

You can request deletion of any data at any time. Since solutions and
preferences live on your device, deleting the app (or using in-app deletion)
removes them. For questions: **support@balkanbit.dev**

## Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, and update the
date above accordingly.
